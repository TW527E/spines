    API

    /badge/name,name.svgReady-made spines for common tools. One lies down, several stand.
    /spine/title.svgAny title, typeset.
    /gh/owner/repo.svgA repository with its stars and language.
    /gh/owner/repo/pull/number.svgA pull request: its title, and its number at the foot. On a shelf, pr:owner/repo/number.
    /gh/owner.svgAn account's most-starred repositories. count 1–12.
    /art/name,name.svgPrints from the gallery.
    /shelf.svg?s=item&s=itemUp to 24 of any kind: a title, badge:name, gh:owner/repo or art:name, each with ;key=value options.

    Options

    theme
    subThe catalog line at the foot.
    iconA Simple Icons slug, or none.
    paper ink accentHex colors, without the #.
    widthslim regular wide
    layoutstack lying down, row standing.
    sizeLength in pixels, 120–2400.
    gapSpace between spines, 0–24.
